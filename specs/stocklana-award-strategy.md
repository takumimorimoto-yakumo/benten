## 1. Objective and fixed boundaries

The recommended thesis is **market readiness from verifiable evidence**. It is explanatory shorthand, not a required product name or graph-shaped UI. The user-confirmed intended primary audience is investors; this is product direction, not measured usage composition. One clear primary flow moves from a company to provider-distinct instruments, rights/reference evidence, verified market economics and an explicit next-action boundary. Operator tooling reuses the same identities and provenance for DBC diagnostics and monitoring, but remains a secondary advanced route that ordinary investors do not have to traverse. The broader capability design remains useful, but the deadline selection is fixed to Meteora, PreStocks and Tessera; that choice is not confirmed eligibility or an award outcome.

The fixed product boundary remains:

- public facts, public chain state, deterministic calculation, and read-only monitoring only;
- no access to private analysis/assets or a private trading/admin data plane; this strategy turn creates or changes no database, while the separately specified future auth/admission stores remain governed by the existing contracts;
- this plan/review performs no custody, login, seed phrase, private key, signature, send, token creation, liquidity funding, order execution or provider launch. A future Clawpump proof may use provider-managed creator-wallet custody or another effect model; it must disclose the verified model and obtain fresh user authority rather than being called noncustodial by default;
- no investment recommendation, “best curve,” revenue optimization, or guaranteed migration;
- unavailable and unverified states stay visible rather than being converted into success.

The deadline product target now includes a non-custodial purchase journey within the Benten experience rather than ending at an external link: conditions/cost review, user-wallet approval, submission state, confirmation or explicit unknown/failure, and resulting-holdings verification. The separate [purchase-complete MVP plan](stocklana-purchase-mvp-execution-plan.md) owns that future implementation and its execution/authority/legal/recovery gates. This plan-only revision creates no such capability and does not relax the current prohibition on server-held keys, signatures, sends, orders or settlement claims.

The strict market boundary is [Market Evidence v1](contracts/market-evidence-v1.md). Provider-distinct xStocks, PreStocks and Tessera references use [Provider Asset Evidence v1](contracts/provider-asset-evidence-v1.md). The [remediation plan](remediation-plan.md), [multi-venue acquisition plan](multi-venue-acquisition-plan.md), and [unsigned acquisition contract](unsigned-acquisition-tool-contract.md) remain the implementation, quote-wire, auth and release SSOTs. This brief does not override them.

## 5. Recommended theme, conditional on evidence

**Recommend Option A as the primary product journey, now completed by one separately gated user-authorized stock-token purchase, with Option B as an opt-in advanced route sharing evidence rather than a co-equal required workflow.** The exact purchase contract and execution order live in the [purchase-complete MVP plan](stocklana-purchase-mvp-execution-plan.md); PreStocks/Tessera remain comparison-only. Option C is later and separately authorized. This preserves operator depth without forcing investor users through configuration tooling or pretending that planning satisfies every prize. The recommendation is conditional on route, wallet, legal, funded-smoke, target-user and sponsor evidence; it is not a winner prediction or launch authorization.

The deadline investor flow is `company -> provider-distinct instrument -> rights/reference evidence -> verified market evidence -> one exact purchase-condition review -> explicit wallet approval -> finality/holding verification`. The current runtime still has no Buy control; only the separately reviewed future implementation may enable it. The submission story begins with that investor problem and value; sponsor integrations are evidence that the flow is real, not four product tabs. OpenAI/Kalshi may show PreStocks and Tessera instruments side by side only when each keeps its own provider, asset kind, mint/contract, rights, reference semantics and source time; they are not purchase targets. Missing SEC/fundamental coverage remains missing. Provider mark/token/auction values are never normalized into an execution quote or issuer/company valuation.

The two views reuse only verified quote-asset identity, Token-2022 state, Scaled UI basis and provenance. They do **not** reuse a market claim. The investor view's DLMM market receives the existing xStock against USDC. The operator view's DBC market pairs a separate base token against the xStock as quote asset. Each market has its own program, pool/config, counterpart mint, roles, economics and rights copy. The DBC-created base token never becomes an xStock or issuer-backed asset merely through that pairing.

The operator consumer is not “another DBC launcher.” It is a no-effect readiness workbench that:

1. selects an allowlisted xStock as a proposed quote token and displays exact mint/program/extension provenance;
2. accepts two explicit operator-supplied DBC scenarios, A and B, without treating either as authority;
3. validates each scenario independently and rejects an invalid scenario with field-specific reasons; only an operator may submit changed values;
4. compares the alternatives by raw configuration values, curve/migration inputs, fee state, quote-token transfer-fee state, Scaled UI display basis, badge/config compatibility, and known/unknown gates—never by “best,” expected return, or revenue;
5. diagnoses mint, badge, raw/scaled amount, fee, net-output, on-chain migration-threshold, and off-chain stock-quote keeper-threshold conditions separately;
6. reads an externally identified existing mainnet DBC pool/config/status with no effect and reports exact slot/time/provenance;
7. exports a strict machine-readable config candidate and a human-readable readiness report with explicit unresolved gates.

The comparison is deterministic for the same A/B inputs, pinned source version, and observation cohort. A/B are explicitly hypothetical operator scenarios; they are never merged with or relabelled as live pool values. The tool never invents, clamps, alters, ranks or recommends a financial setting.

Mandatory validation checklist:

- generic SDK config validation is not quote-mint or badge validation;
- base-side transfer-hook handling is distinct from quote-side hook support, which remains unproved and blocking when active;
- SDK `marketCap` is in quote-token units, not automatic USD, and a multiplier alone is not a price;
- a DBC-created new base token does not inherit the xStock issuer's identity, backing, equity, or redemption rights merely because an xStock is the quote asset.

A stock DBC config cannot force an arbitrary USD-oracle or corporate-event migration condition. On-chain curve completion, off-chain keeper eligibility, and existence of the migrated DAMM v2 pool remain three separate states.

DLMM remains a separate acquisition component. If Option B is selected, its positive quote can explain how a user may inspect secondary-market xStock availability or how an operator may reason about obtaining the quote asset. It cannot prove DBC creation, graduation, DAMM v2 liquidity, or keeper behavior.
